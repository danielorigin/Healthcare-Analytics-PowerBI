|  # | Measure                          | DAX Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Purpose                                                                                                                                          |

| -: | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

|  1 | \*\*Length of Stay\*\*               | `DATEDIFF('Admissions'\[Date of Admission], 'Admissions'\[Discharge Date], DAY)`                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Calculates the number of days between admission and discharge.                                                                                   |

|  2 | \*\*Negative Billing Amount\*\*      | `CALCULATE(SUM('Billing Info'\[Billing Amount]), 'Billing Info'\[Billing Amount] < 0)`                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Calculates the total value of negative billing amounts.                                                                                          |

|  3 | \*\*Negative Billing Count\*\*       | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Billing Info'), 'Billing Info'\[Billing Amount] < 0)`                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Counts billing records with negative amounts.                                                                                                    |

|  4 | \*\*Admissions Count\*\*             | `COUNT('Healthcare Dataset'\[Date of Admission])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Counts admission records.                                                                                                                        |

|  5 | \*\*Admissions per Patient\*\*       | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Healthcare Dataset'), ALLEXCEPT('Healthcare Dataset', 'Healthcare Dataset'\[Name]))`                                                                                                                                                                                              | Counts records/admissions associated with each patient.                                                                                          |

|  6 | \*\*Average Age\*\*                  | `AVERAGE('Patient Details'\[Age])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Calculates the average patient age.                                                                                                              |

|  7 | \*\*Average Stay\*\*                 | `AVERAGE('Admissions'\[Length of Stay])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Calculates the average length of hospital stay.                                                                                                  |

|  8 | \*\*Avg Billing\*\*                  | `AVERAGE('Billing Info'\[Billing Amount])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Calculates the average billing amount.                                                                                                           |

|  9 | \*\*Avg Billing per Patient\*\*      | `AVERAGEX(VALUES('Healthcare Dataset'\[Name]), CALCULATE(SUM('Billing Info'\[Billing Amount])))`                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Calculates the average total billing amount per unique patient.                                                                                  |

| 10 | \*\*Bed Occupancy Rate\*\*           | `DIVIDE(\[Occupied Beds], 500, 0)`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Calculates occupied beds as a percentage of 500 available beds.                                                                                  |

| 11 | \*\*Diagnosis Count\*\*              | `DISTINCTCOUNT('Healthcare Dataset'\[Medical Condition])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Counts the number of unique medical conditions.                                                                                                  |

| 12 | \*\*Doctor Workload\*\*              | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Healthcare Dataset'), ALLEXCEPT('Healthcare Dataset', 'Healthcare Dataset'\[Doctor]))`                                                                                                                                                                                            | Counts records associated with each doctor.                                                                                                      |

| 13 | \*\*Estimated Outstanding Amount\*\* | `CALCULATE(SUM('Billing Info'\[Billing Amount]), 'Billing Info'\[Billing Amount] < 0)`                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Calculates the sum of negative billing values, treated as outstanding amounts.                                                                   |

| 14 | \*\*Estimated Paid Amount\*\*        | `CALCULATE(SUM('Billing Info'\[Billing Amount]))`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Calculates the total billing amount. \*\*Note:\*\* this does not necessarily represent actual paid amounts unless the column represents payments.    |

| 15 | \*\*Highest Billing\*\*              | `MAX('Billing Info'\[Billing Amount])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Finds the highest billing amount.                                                                                                                |

| 16 | \*\*Insured Patients\*\*             | `CALCULATE(\[Total Patients], NOT(ISBLANK('Healthcare Dataset'\[Insurance Provider])))`                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Counts patients with an insurance provider recorded.                                                                                             |

| 17 | \*\*Length of Stay (Days)\*\*        | `DATEDIFF('Healthcare Dataset'\[Date of Admission], 'Healthcare Dataset'\[Discharge Date], DAY)`                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Calculates length of stay directly from the Healthcare Dataset.                                                                                  |

| 18 | \*\*Lowest Diagnosis\*\*             | `VAR SummaryTable = SUMMARIZE('Medical Info', 'Medical Info'\[Medical Condition], "ConditionCount", COUNTROWS('Medical Info'))`<br><br>`VAR LowestCondition = TOPN(1, SummaryTable, \[ConditionCount], ASC)`<br><br>`RETURN CONCATENATEX(LowestCondition, 'Medical Info'\[Medical Condition], ", ")`      | Identifies the medical condition with the lowest number of records.                                                                              |

| 19 | \*\*Median Age\*\*                   | `MEDIAN('Healthcare Dataset'\[Age])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Calculates the median patient age.                                                                                                               |

| 20 | \*\*Medication Count\*\*             | `DISTINCTCOUNT('Healthcare Dataset'\[Medication])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Counts unique medications.                                                                                                                       |

| 21 | \*\*Most Frequent Condition\*\*      | `VAR SummaryTable = SUMMARIZE('Healthcare Dataset', 'Healthcare Dataset'\[Medical Condition], "ConditionCount", COUNTROWS('Healthcare Dataset'))`<br><br>`VAR TopCondition = TOPN(1, SummaryTable, \[ConditionCount], DESC)`<br><br>`RETURN MAXX(TopCondition, 'Healthcare Dataset'\[Medical Condition])` | Identifies the most frequently recorded medical condition.                                                                                       |

| 22 | \*\*Occupied Beds\*\*                | `DISTINCTCOUNT('Healthcare Dataset'\[Room Number])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Counts the number of distinct occupied rooms/beds.                                                                                               |

| 23 | \*\*Readmission Rate\*\*             | `DIVIDE(\[Total Admissions by date], \[Unique Patients], 0)`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Calculates admissions relative to unique patients. \*\*This is not a true readmission rate unless admissions are specifically repeat admissions.\*\* |

| 24 | \*\*Top Diagnosis\*\*                | `VAR SummaryTable = SUMMARIZE('Healthcare Dataset', 'Healthcare Dataset'\[Medical Condition], "ConditionCount", COUNTROWS('Healthcare Dataset'))`<br><br>`VAR TopCondition = TOPN(1, SummaryTable, \[ConditionCount], DESC)`<br><br>`RETURN MAXX(TopCondition, 'Healthcare Dataset'\[Medical Condition])` | Identifies the most frequently recorded diagnosis/condition.                                                                                     |

| 25 | \*\*Total Admissions\*\*             | `\[Admissions Count] - \[Total Discharges]`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Calculates admissions minus discharges. \*\*This is not normally a total admissions measure.\*\*                                                     |

| 26 | \*\*Total Admissions by Date\*\*     | `COUNT('Admissions'\[Admission Year])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Counts admission-year records.                                                                                                                   |

| 27 | \*\*Total Billing\*\*                | `SUM('Billing Info'\[Billing Amount])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Calculates total billing.                                                                                                                        |

| 28 | \*\*Total Diagnoses\*\*              | `DISTINCTCOUNT('Medical Info'\[Medical Condition])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Counts unique diagnoses/medical conditions.                                                                                                      |

| 29 | \*\*Total Discharges\*\*             | `COUNTROWS(FILTER('Admissions', NOT(ISBLANK('Admissions'\[Discharge Date]))))`                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Counts records with a discharge date.                                                                                                            |

| 30 | \*\*Total Invoices\*\*               | `COUNT('Billing Info'\[Billing Amount])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Counts billing records containing a billing amount.                                                                                              |

| 31 | \*\*Total Patients\*\*               | `COUNT('Healthcare Dataset'\[Name])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Counts patient-name records, \*\*not unique patients\*\*.                                                                                            |

| 32 | \*\*Unique Patients\*\*              | `DISTINCTCOUNT('Healthcare Dataset'\[Name])`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Counts unique patient names.                                                                                                                     |
